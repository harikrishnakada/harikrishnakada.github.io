# About Me

I'm a **Senior Software Developer** at Digital Infuzion, LLC., with approximately **8 years of experience** building enterprise-scale applications across the full technology stack. My expertise spans Backend, Front-End, DevOps, CI/CD, Agentic AI, and Cloud technologies.

## Professional Summary

I specialize in developing and maintaining **clinical research management platforms** that enable real-time tracking and reporting of clinical research data and workflows. My work has a direct impact on advancing medical research at prestigious institutions including the National Institute of Aging (NIA) and the National Institute of Dental and Craniofacial Research (NIDCR).

### Key Strengths

- **Full-Stack Expertise**: Proficient in building APIs, asynchronous services, event-based services, long-running services, and web applications
- **AI Innovation**: Hands-on experience with Large Language Models (LLMs), Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG), and Agentic AI frameworks
- **Forward Deployed Engineering**: Direct collaboration with stakeholders to understand business needs and deploy intelligent solutions
- **Leadership**: Proven track record in mentoring team members and contributing to organizational AI initiatives

## What Drives Me

I'm passionate about leveraging cutting-edge AI and cloud technologies to solve complex problems in healthcare and clinical research. I believe in the power of intelligent automation to enhance productivity, streamline workflows, and improve decision-making in complex systems.

Currently, I'm actively:
- Contributing to Digital Infuzion's AI initiatives
- Mentoring interns as part of the AI Incubator Program
- Exploring innovative applications of LLMs and agentic AI in clinical research
